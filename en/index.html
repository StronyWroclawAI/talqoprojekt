Teaching method

How we teach and why.

We teach so that language is a tool, not a school subject. Every lesson starts with conversation and ends with something you can use that same day.

We work on real situations: a job interview, a message to a client, ordering in a restaurant, a school presentation. Grammar and vocabulary appear when they are needed – not the other way round.

After each lesson you receive a short summary and materials for independent revision.

  • Live lessons with a teacher

    Every lesson takes place online, in real time.

  • Conversation first

    Small groups leave more time for speaking.

  • Real situations

    We teach language that is useful at work, when travelling and day to day.

  • Placement test to begin

    It allows us to match the course to your current level.

About TALQO

TALQO is an online language school. We teach English, German and Italian – to children, teenagers, students, adults, seniors and company teams.

What matters most to us: quality and care, experience, honesty and an individual approach – at an accessible price.

Behind every course there is a teacher who knows your goal: an exam, a promotion or a dream trip. That is why we start with a conversation and a placement test, and only then propose a learning plan.

Participant safety

Online lessons need clear rules – especially when children and teenagers are learning.

  • Personal data protection

    We process data in line with the GDPR and only to handle enquiries and lessons. Details are set out in the privacy policy.

  • Lessons for children and teenagers

    A minor is enrolled by a parent or guardian. A parent may be present during the lesson, and contact outside lessons takes place only with the guardian.

  • Safe online meetings

    Lessons are joined through an individual link with a waiting room. We do not record lessons without the explicit consent of the participant or guardian.
