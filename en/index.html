From enquiry to first lesson – four steps.

  1. 1

    Request a date

    You fill in the short “Request a date” form – just the language and your preferred lesson time.

  2. 2

    We get back to you

    We call or write back to learn about your goal and answer your questions.

  3. 3

    Placement test

    We check your current level and suggest a course and lesson format.

  4. 4

    First online lesson

    You meet your teacher at the agreed time.

Frequently asked questions

Are lessons held online only?

Yes. All lessons are held online, live with a teacher.

Which languages does TALQO teach?

English, German and Italian.

Who are the courses for?

For preschool children, primary and secondary school pupils (including exam preparation), university students, adults, seniors and companies.

When can I enrol?

Enrolment is open all year round. Simply send the “Request a date” form – we will call or write back.

How do I find out my level?

Before lessons begin we carry out a placement test, which lets us choose the right course.

How much do lessons cost and how can I pay?

Current prices and lesson packages are listed in the price list, and the available payment options in the “Payment methods” section.
